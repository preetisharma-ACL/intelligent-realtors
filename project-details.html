Project overview

The Aravalli Residences sits on a 12.5-acre parcel along Golf Course Extension Road, one of Gurugram’s most established residential corridors. With just four towers and 420 homes, it keeps density low and open space generous — nearly 78% of the site is landscaped.

Every residence is a corner home with three-side ventilation, a 10-foot ceiling and a private lift lobby. Wraparound decks open onto views of the Aravalli ridge, and the 5 BHK sky villas add a private terrace garden.
