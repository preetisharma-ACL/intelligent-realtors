Last updated: 14 October 2026 Applies to: intelligentrealtors.in RERA No. UPRERAAGT10452

Intelligent Realtors Advisory LLP (“Intelligent Realtors”, “we”, “us”) is a RERA-registered real estate advisory. This policy explains how we handle personal data when you visit our website, fill in a form, call or message us, visit a site with us, or apply for a job.

We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them. By using this website or sharing your details with us, you agree to the practices described here.

01Who we are

The data fiduciary responsible for your personal data is Intelligent Realtors Advisory LLP, A-14, Second Floor, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201301, registered with UP RERA as a real estate agent under registration number UPRERAAGT10452.

Questions about this policy can be sent to connect@intelligentrealtors.in.

02Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiry details: your name, mobile number, email address, city, and what you are looking for, such as property type, budget, locality and possession timeline.
  • Conversations: notes from calls, WhatsApp chats, emails and meetings with our advisors, including your stated preferences and feedback on properties you have seen.
  • Transaction support: where you ask us to help with a booking, home loan or registry, the documents needed for it, such as identity and address proof, PAN and, for NRI clients, passport and power-of-attorney details.
  • Job applications: your CV, contact details, experience and anything else you choose to send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, IP address, approximate location, pages visited, time spent and the site that referred you, collected through cookies and similar tools.
  • Campaign information, such as the advertisement or link that brought you to us.

Information from others

  • Enquiries passed to us by developers we represent, property portals or people who refer you to us.

03How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and assign an advisor who can call or message you back.
  • To shortlist properties that fit your brief, arrange site visits and share brochures, floor plans, price lists and payment plans.
  • To register you with a developer for a project you are interested in, so that your booking is tracked to us as the channel partner.
  • To help with loans, documentation, registry and handover, where you ask us to.
  • To send updates on new launches and market notes, where you have agreed to receive them.
  • To assess job applications and contact candidates.
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including RERA, tax and anti-money-laundering requirements, and to resolve disputes.

04Consent and legal basis

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you submit a form, call or message us, or otherwise share your details with us. Where the law allows, we also process data for legitimate uses such as complying with a legal obligation or responding to a request you have made.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to connect@intelligentrealtors.in. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it, but it may mean we can no longer help with your property search.

05Calls, SMS, WhatsApp and email

When you submit an enquiry, you authorise us to contact you about it by phone call, SMS, WhatsApp and email, even if your number is registered on the National Do Not Call registry. We contact you only about property you have asked about, related projects and our services.

To stop marketing messages, reply STOP on WhatsApp or SMS, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or tell your advisor. We will still contact you about an active transaction where needed.

06Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed to provide our services:

  • Developers whose projects you are interested in, so they can register your enquiry, confirm availability and process a booking.
  • Banks and housing finance companies, only if you ask us to help with a home loan.
  • Lawyers, valuers and documentation agents, only where you ask for help with due diligence, registry or other paperwork.
  • Service providers who work on our behalf, such as CRM, telephony, messaging, email, hosting and analytics providers, under contracts that limit how they may use your data.
  • Authorities, where required by law, court order or a regulator such as RERA.

Once a developer, bank or other third party receives your details, their own privacy policy also applies to how they use them.

07Cookies and analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep it working, remember your preferences, measure traffic and understand which pages and campaigns are useful. Some cookies are set by third parties, such as analytics and advertising providers.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. The site will still work, although some features, such as remembered search filters, may not.

08How long we keep it

  • Enquiries are kept for up to 3 years from your last interaction with us, so we can continue helping you if your search resumes.
  • Transaction records are kept for as long as tax, RERA and other laws require, which is usually 8 years.
  • Job applications are kept for up to 12 months, unless you join us or ask us to delete them sooner.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it.

09How we protect it

We use reasonable security practices to protect your data. Access is limited to staff who need it for their work, devices and accounts are password protected, and service providers are chosen with security in mind.

No method of sending or storing data is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

10Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected or updated;
  • have your data erased when it is no longer needed for the purpose you gave it for;
  • withdraw consent you have given;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to do so; and
  • have your grievances addressed by us, and then by the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

To use any of these rights, email connect@intelligentrealtors.in. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We aim to respond within 30 days.

11Children

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has shared their details with us, please contact us and we will delete them.

12NRI clients and data outside India

If you contact us from outside India, your data will be processed and stored in India. Some of our service providers may store data in other countries. Where that happens, we transfer data only as permitted under Indian law.

13Links to other websites

Our website links to developer websites, maps and social media. We are not responsible for how those sites handle your data, so please read their privacy policies.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last changed at the top. If we make significant changes, we will tell you by email or through a notice on the website.

15Grievance officer and contact

If you have a question or complaint about how we handle your personal data, please contact our Grievance Officer. We will acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within 30 days.
