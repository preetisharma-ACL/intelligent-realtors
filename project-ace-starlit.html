Frequently asked questions

ACE Starlit is a residential apartment project by ACE Group in Sector 152, Noida, offering premium 2 BHK and 3 BHK residences.

The project is located at SC-01/09, Sector 152, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201310, near the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway.

The project is marketed under ACE Group. The UP RERA record identifies Star Landcraft Private Limited as the registered promoter.

The UP RERA registration number is UPRERAPRJ677294. Buyers should check the official UP RERA record for the latest project status and disclosures.

The project offers 2 BHK and 3 BHK apartments, with two listed layout variants for each configuration. Availability of individual units should be confirmed with the sales team.

A third-party listing shows an indicative starting price of approximately ₹1.99 crore. The current price depends on unit availability, floor, view and additional charges.

The advertised super-area range is approximately 1,350–1,775 sq.ft. Carpet areas have not been independently confirmed.

The project is reported to have five towers and 492 apartments.

The project covers approximately 6.79 acres, according to property listings.

The UP RERA record lists 30 April 2025 as the declared completion date. Some property portals list possession from October 2025. Confirm actual handover status directly with the developer.

Some property portals classify ACE Starlit as ready to move. Since the registered completion date alone does not confirm actual possession of every unit, verify the relevant completion or occupancy certificate and unit handover status.

Advertised amenities include a swimming pool, clubhouse, gymnasium, landscaped areas, jogging and recreational facilities, security and parking. Confirm the final operational amenities list before booking.

Noida Sector 148 Metro Station on the Aqua Line is one of the nearest stations. Listings place it approximately 0.6–1.3 km away, depending on the source and measurement method.

The project offers multi-bedroom apartments, recreational amenities and access to schools, hospitals and metro connectivity. Suitability depends on the buyer&#x27;s budget, commuting requirements and the amenities available at the time of possession.
